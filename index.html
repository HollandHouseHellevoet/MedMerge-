The Network

Your employees are the healthcare. That changes everything.

Medical practices are unique. The people on your health plan are the same people delivering care.

A consult that costs a carrier $350 at retail costs your practice $150 to deliver. Inside the network, plan members get care at cost, not retail. That $200 difference on a single visit is not a discount. It's the structural economics of cutting out the middleman.

Multiply that across every service rendered between member practices. The savings compound as the network grows. Live today in Phoenix, Dallas, Manhattan (KS), and Tulsa.

Domestic Network

Care at your own facility at cost. Zero cost share for employees.

Friends & Family

Care from any member practice at cost, not retail. $350 becomes $150.

Intrinsic Savings

Lower actual cost of care, not negotiated discounts. Compounds with every practice that joins.
