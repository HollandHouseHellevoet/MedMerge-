The Structure

How MedMerge Works

What is MedMerge?

MedMerge is a single employer platform for medical practices. We become the employer of record and lease your employees back to you. Your practice keeps full operational control. The structure gives practices of any size access to self-funded healthcare benefits, payroll, and HR on one platform.

What does "single employer platform" mean?

MedMerge becomes the legal employer of record for your staff. Your employees are leased back to you, and you retain complete operational control: hiring, firing, scheduling, clinical decisions, and day-to-day management. What changes is the infrastructure underneath. Benefits, payroll, HR, and compliance move to a platform built for medical practices.

How is MedMerge different from a PEO?

A PEO shares operational control with you, pools your employees into a fully insured plan, and typically requires 5 to 10 employees minimum. MedMerge keeps operational control entirely with the practice, provides self-funded benefits starting at one employee, and gives you ownership of your claims data. You are not pooled with unrelated businesses.

Do I lose control of my practice?

No. You keep full operational control. MedMerge handles benefits administration, payroll processing, HR compliance, and the infrastructure that supports those functions. Clinical decisions, staffing, scheduling, and practice management remain yours.

What size practice can use MedMerge?

Any medical practice with fewer than 150 employees. There is no minimum. The platform is designed to give a one-employee practice the same benefits infrastructure available to large health systems.

Benefits & Network

Self-Funded Healthcare

What is SurgRE?

SurgRE is a self-funded benefits program owned by its physician members and designed exclusively for healthcare professionals. By joining SurgRE through MedMerge, your practice moves from fully insured to self-funded. You own your claims data, you control your plan design, and when claims come in under projections, the surplus stays with the members rather than going to a carrier.

What is the Friends & Family network?

When plan members receive care from another member practice, they pay the practice's actual cost of delivery, not the retail rate. A consult that costs $350 at retail but $150 to deliver is billed to the plan at $150. No copays, no deductibles, no coinsurance. These are structural savings, not negotiated discounts. The more practices in the network, the more services stay inside it, and the lower plan expenses go for everyone.

What is the domestic network?

Your employees receive care at your own facility at cost. The plan pays your actual cost of delivery. Plan expense drops, and revenue stays in-house.

Where is the network available?

The Friends & Family network is live and growing in four markets: Phoenix, Dallas, Manhattan (Kansas), and Tulsa. The platform itself serves practices nationwide. Network savings are strongest where member practice density is highest.

Can my employees still see doctors outside the network?

Yes. The Friends & Family network provides the lowest-cost care option, but employees are not restricted to it. Care outside the network is covered under the plan's standard terms.

What happens if we have a catastrophic claim?

SurgRE includes stop-loss protection. Catastrophic claims are absorbed by the collective, not by your practice alone. The structure provides financial predictability without giving up the savings of self-funding.

Pricing & Tiers

Cost and Service Levels

How much does MedMerge cost?

Pricing depends on your practice size, employee count, and the tier you select. All three tiers include the single employer structure and self-funded healthcare benefits. Contact us for a quote specific to your practice.

What is included in every tier?

Every tier includes the single employer structure, self-funded healthcare benefits through SurgRE, and access to the Friends & Family network. Tiers differ by the level of payroll, HR, and talent management services included.

Do I need to choose Tier 3 to get healthcare benefits?

No. Healthcare benefits are included in every tier. Tier 1 covers payroll and compliance. Tier 2 adds HR foundation. Tier 3 adds talent management, compensation management, and custom integrations. The benefits structure is the same across all three.

Getting Started

Implementation

How long does implementation take?

Implementation timelines vary by practice size and complexity. A typical onboarding takes 30 to 60 days. We handle the transition of payroll, benefits enrollment, and compliance setup.

What happens to our current benefits plan?

Your practice transitions from its current fully insured plan to self-funded benefits through SurgRE. We coordinate the timing so there is no gap in coverage for your employees.

Is there a contract term?

Contact us for details on agreement terms. We structure arrangements to align with your practice's needs and the plan year cycle.

What if we want to leave MedMerge?

Your practice can transition off the platform. Employees transfer back to your direct employment. We work with you to ensure continuity of benefits and payroll during the transition.

Do we need to change our payroll schedule or processes?

No. MedMerge supports your existing payroll schedule and direct deposit setup. The transition is designed to be transparent to your employees.
