The Platform

What MedMerge Is

What is MedMerge?

MedMerge is a single employer platform built for medical practices. We become the employer of record for your staff and lease them back to your practice. You keep the practice, the name on the building, and every operating and clinical decision.

What moves to MedMerge is the infrastructure underneath: self-funded health benefits, payroll, HR, and compliance. One platform, one point of contact, large-group economics.

Why do independent practices pay more for benefits?

Because they are small. Not because they are inefficient.

A 12-person practice and a 12,000-person health system buy the same things: health benefits, workers' compensation, payroll, HR. The system pays 25 to 40% less for substantially the same coverage. Large employers self-fund and control where their employees get care. Small practices buy fully insured coverage at retail and send their employees into a network priced for someone else's margin.

We call this the Independence Tax. It is structural, it compounds every year, and it is the economic pressure that pushes practices toward private equity or hospital employment. MedMerge gives independent practices the cost structure of a large employer without asking them to sell, merge, or give up ownership.

Do I lose control of my practice?

No. MedMerge does not take equity, does not take a board seat, and does not touch clinical operations. You decide staffing, scheduling, compensation, clinical protocols, payer strategy, and how you run the business.

What size practice can use MedMerge?

Any independent medical practice from one employee up. There is no minimum. The standard platform is built for practices under 150 employees.

Benefits & Network

How It Lowers Costs

What is the difference between fully insured and self-funded?

Control.

In a fully insured plan, a carrier sets your rates, writes your plan design, decides your network, and tells you very little about where the money went. Every renewal is a number you did not choose and cannot challenge.

In a self-funded plan, the plan pays claims directly. Plan design is built for the people on it. Claims reporting shows where care is going and what it costs. Large employers have run this way for decades. Small practices were locked out because they could not carry the risk alone. MedMerge is how an independent practice makes that move.

What is the domestic network?

Your own people receive care at your own facility. The plan pays your actual cost of delivery, and your employee pays nothing out of pocket.

Two things happen at once. Plan expense drops, because the plan pays cost instead of retail. And the care, the relationship, and the revenue stay inside your practice instead of going to a competitor or a hospital system.

What is the Friends & Family network?

When a plan member receives care at another member practice, the plan pays that practice its actual cost of delivery, not the retail rate. A consult billed at $350 retail that costs $150 to deliver is paid at $150. No copay, no deductible, no coinsurance.

These are structural savings, not negotiated discounts. Every practice that joins adds services the network can deliver at cost. The more specialties inside the network, the more care stays inside it, and the lower plan expense goes for everyone.

Where is the network available?

The platform serves practices nationwide. The domestic network is available everywhere, because it is your own practice. The Friends & Family network is live in four markets: Phoenix, Dallas, Manhattan (Kansas), and Tulsa.

Can members still see doctors outside the network?

Yes. The domestic and Friends & Family networks are the lowest-cost option, not the only option. Care outside them is covered under the plan's standard terms.

What happens if we have a catastrophic claim?

Your practice does not carry it alone. Claims are paid from the plan, not from your practice's operating account. Above a set threshold, specific stop-loss coverage pays the excess. Aggregate stop-loss caps total plan claims for the year.

Pricing & Tiers

Cost and Service Levels

How much does MedMerge cost?

Pricing depends on your employee count, the claims profile of your workforce, and the service tier you choose. Every quote is built on your actual numbers, side by side with what you pay today.

The comparison that matters is total cost: what you spend now on premiums, payroll services, HR, and compliance, against what you spend inside MedMerge. Contact us for a practice-specific quote.

What is included in every tier?

Every tier includes the single employer structure, self-funded health benefits, access to the domestic and Friends & Family networks, payroll processing and tax filing, and employment compliance. Tiers differ in how much HR and talent support comes with it.

Do I need Tier 3 to get healthcare benefits?

No. The benefits are identical in every tier. Tiers change the HR service level, not the coverage your people receive.

Getting Started

Implementation

When can we join?

Anytime. Fully insured plans are written month to month, so you do not have to wait for a renewal date to leave your carrier.

How long does implementation take?

90 days. That covers benefits underwriting, payroll and HR setup, open enrollment, and the move of your employees to the MedMerge employing entity.

What happens to our current benefits plan?

It ends on the day MedMerge coverage begins, timed so there is no gap in coverage. If you are fully insured today, the transition is clean: you give your carrier notice, your old coverage stops, MedMerge starts.

What changes for our employees?

Less than you would expect. Their manager, their schedule, their job, and their paycheck timing stay the same. What changes: their W-2 comes from the MedMerge employing entity, they complete standard new-employer paperwork, and they enroll in new benefits during an open enrollment we run with them. They also gain care with no out-of-pocket cost at your practice and across the Friends & Family network.

What if we want to leave MedMerge?

You can. Your employees transfer back to your practice's direct employment, and we coordinate payroll, benefits continuation, and records so the transition does not interrupt anyone's paycheck or coverage.
