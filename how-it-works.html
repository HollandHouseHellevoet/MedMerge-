How It Works

One structure. Three capabilities. Total independence.

MedMerge is a single employer platform. We become the employer of record for your staff, lease them back to you, and give your practice access to self-funded benefits, payroll, and HR — regardless of your size.

The Structure

You keep control. We handle the infrastructure.

You tried a PEO. It felt like giving up your practice. The single employer platform works differently.

MedMerge becomes the employer of record and leases your employees back to you. You retain full operational control — hiring, firing, schedules, clinical decisions. What changes is the infrastructure underneath: benefits, payroll, HR, and compliance move to a platform designed for medical practices.

PEO

  • Shared operational control
  • Fully insured pool
  • 5-10+ employee minimum
  • Carrier owns claims data
  • Limited plan design

MedMerge

  • Your operational control
  • Self-funded captive
  • 1 employee minimum
  • You own claims data
  • Full plan flexibility
The Captive

Self-funded benefits. Without the 50-employee minimum.

SurgRE is a homogeneous captive insurance entity designed exclusively for healthcare professionals. By joining SurgRE through MedMerge, practices of any size access self-funded benefits with the collective purchasing power of the entire captive.

Collective Purchasing Power

Lower costs for healthcare services, prescription drugs, and plan components across the entire captive membership.

Risk Mitigation

A robust stop-loss layer protecting against unexpected catastrophic claims. Financial stability without giving up the savings of self-funding.

Claims Transparency

You own your data. Full visibility into where every dollar goes. No more black-box carrier reporting.

Plan Design Flexibility

Tailor your plan to your workforce instead of accepting the carrier's one-size-fits-all offering.

The Network

No copays. No deductibles. No coinsurance. Just care.

The Friends & Family network is the structural advantage no other platform can offer.

Domestic Network

Employees receive care at their own facility with zero cost share. No copays, no deductibles, no coinsurance. Plan expense drops. Revenue stays in-house. Employee satisfaction goes up.

Friends & Family Network

When a plan member visits another practice in the captive, they receive care at deeply discounted rates. The provider gets paid directly. The plan saves on claims.

Intrinsic Savings

Every service rendered inside the network reduces the cost basis for everyone. The more the network grows, the more everyone saves. This is not a discount card. It is a structural economic advantage that compounds.

Stop paying the carrier to use your own doctors.

Blue Cross, United, Cigna, Aetna — you know the names. You also know their business model depends on keeping you dependent. MedMerge's infrastructure liberates your practice from traditional insurance networks. More control. Less bureaucracy. Your pricing.
