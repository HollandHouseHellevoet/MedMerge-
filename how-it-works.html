The Network

No copays. No deductibles. No coinsurance. Just care.

The Friends & Family network is the structural advantage no other platform can offer. Here's why.

Say a knee injection costs a carrier $900 at retail. The practice delivers it for $275. Inside the network, your fellow plan members get it at cost. $275. The plan just saved $625 on a single procedure. No negotiation. No middleman. No carrier markup.

Now multiply that across every service rendered between member practices. Every visit inside the network costs less than the same visit outside it. The savings are structural, not negotiated. They compound as the network grows.

Domestic Network

Your employees receive care at your own facility at cost. No copays, no deductibles, no coinsurance. The plan pays your actual cost of delivery, not a carrier's retail rate. Plan expense drops. Revenue stays in-house.

Friends & Family Network

When a plan member visits another practice in SurgRE, they receive care at that practice's cost, not retail. A specialist visit billed at $400 becomes $120. A procedure billed at $6,000 retail might cost $2,400 to deliver. The provider gets paid directly. The plan saves on every claim.

Intrinsic Savings

These cost reductions are not discounts. They are the difference between retail pricing and actual cost of care. Every service rendered inside the network lowers overall plan expenses for every member. The more practices in the network, the more services stay inside it, and the cheaper healthcare gets for everyone.

Where the Network Is Strongest

The Friends & Family network is live and growing in four markets: Phoenix, Dallas, Manhattan (Kansas), and Tulsa. Network density drives savings. The more member practices in a market, the more care stays inside the network. If your practice is in one of these markets, the economics are already working.
