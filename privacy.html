Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

MedMerge, LLC ("MedMerge," "we," "us," or "our") is a Texas limited liability company. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when visiting medmerge.co (the "Site") or engaging with our services.

Information We Collect

Website Visitors

When you request a demo, subscribe to communications, or contact us through the Site, we may collect:

  • Your name and professional title
  • Your practice or facility name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your medical specialty
  • The approximate number of employees at your facility
  • Your approximate annual insurance and benefits expenditure

We also collect information automatically when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and referring URLs. This information is collected through standard analytics tools including Google Analytics.

Platform Participants

When your practice joins MedMerge as a member, we collect and process additional information necessary to administer employment, payroll, benefits, and compliance. This includes employee names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, compensation, tax withholding elections, benefits enrollment, and related employment records. This information is collected directly from the practice and from employees during onboarding and benefits enrollment.

Protected Health Information (HIPAA)

MedMerge serves as the employer of record for member practices and sponsors a self-funded group health plan. In this capacity, MedMerge is subject to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations.

Protected Health Information (PHI) — individually identifiable health information related to plan enrollment, claims, and benefits administration — is handled in accordance with the HIPAA Privacy Rule and Security Rule.

  • We use PHI only for plan administration, payment, and healthcare operations as permitted by HIPAA.
  • We do not use PHI for marketing.
  • We do not sell PHI.
  • We execute Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with third-party service providers who access PHI on our behalf. These agreements require our business associates to safeguard PHI and limit its use to the services they perform for us.

Plan participants may exercise their HIPAA rights — including the right to access, amend, and request an accounting of disclosures of their PHI — by contacting us at the address below. We will respond in accordance with applicable HIPAA requirements.

A separate Notice of Privacy Practices describing your rights under HIPAA in detail is provided to plan participants at enrollment.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to demo requests and inquiries
  • Administer employment, payroll, and benefits for member practices
  • Send communications you have requested
  • Improve and optimize the Site and our services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in operating the Site and delivering our services, including payroll processing, benefits administration, email delivery, analytics, and form processing providers. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Plan administration as necessary for the operation of the group health plan, including claims processing, network administration, and stop-loss coverage.
  • Legal requirements when we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MedMerge, our clients, or others.

We do not share your information for marketing purposes with any third party.

Analytics and Tracking

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Google Analytics collects data including pages viewed, time on site, and general geographic location. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

We do not use advertising tracking pixels or retargeting technologies on this Site.

Cookies

The Site uses cookies to support analytics and to remember your preferences. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies or to notify you when cookies are set. Disabling cookies may affect certain Site functionality.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to maintain our business records, and to comply with applicable law. Employment and benefits records are retained in accordance with applicable federal and state requirements. You may request deletion of your website-submitted information at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. For HIPAA-protected health information, your rights are governed by the HIPAA Privacy Rule and described in our Notice of Privacy Practices. To make any request, contact us at Info@MedMerge.co. We will respond within 30 days or as required by applicable law.

If you have subscribed to communications from us, you may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any communication or by contacting us directly.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. For PHI, we maintain the administrative, physical, and technical safeguards required by the HIPAA Security Rule. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Site following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law provisions. Any disputes arising under this policy shall be resolved in the courts of Texas.
