Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: April 2026

MedMerge, LLC ("MedMerge," "we," "us," or "our") is a Texas limited liability company. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when visiting medmerge.co (the "Site") or engaging with our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us. When you submit a request for a Practice Economics Report, subscribe to communications, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name and professional title
  • Your practice or facility name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your medical specialty
  • The approximate number of employees at your facility
  • Your approximate annual insurance and benefits expenditure

We also collect information automatically when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and referring URLs. This information is collected through standard analytics tools including Google Analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Prepare and deliver your personalized Practice Economics Report
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Send you practice economics briefs and communications you have requested
  • Improve and optimize the Site and our services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in operating the Site and delivering our services, including email delivery, analytics, and form processing providers. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements when we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MedMerge, our clients, or others.

We do not share your information for marketing purposes with any third party.

Practice Economics Reports

Information you provide for the purpose of receiving a Practice Economics Report is used exclusively to prepare that report and to follow up with you regarding MedMerge services. Report contents are treated as confidential. We do not disclose the contents of your report or the identifying details of your practice to any third party without your explicit written consent.

Analytics and Tracking

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Google Analytics collects data including pages viewed, time on site, and general geographic location. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

We do not use advertising tracking pixels or retargeting technologies on this Site.

Cookies

The Site uses cookies to support analytics and to remember your preferences. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies or to notify you when cookies are set. Disabling cookies may affect certain Site functionality.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to maintain our business records, and to comply with applicable law. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us at Info@MedMerge.co. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

If you have subscribed to communications from us, you may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any communication or by contacting us directly.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Site following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law provisions. Any disputes arising under this policy shall be resolved in the courts of Texas.
