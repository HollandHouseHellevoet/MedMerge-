Healthcare Benefits

Your benefits plan should work as hard as your practice. It doesn't.

Fully insured plans are designed for the carrier's benefit, not yours. MedMerge gives medical practices of any size access to self-funded healthcare benefits through the SurgRE captive.

The Problem

Where your premium dollar goes. And what you never get back.

Every dollar you pay in premiums gets carved up before a single claim is paid. The carrier takes premium taxes, risk charges, administrative fees, and profit margin. If claims come in low, the carrier keeps the surplus. If the float generates income, the carrier keeps that too.

You have no visibility into claims data, no control over plan design, and no leverage at renewal. The broker calls this 'the market.' It is not the market. It is the structure, and you are on the wrong side of it.

The Solution

Self-funded benefits. Starting at one employee.

Through MedMerge's single employer structure, your practice joins the SurgRE captive and transitions from fully insured to self-funded.

You Own Your Claims Data

Full visibility into where every dollar goes. No more opaque carrier reports.

You Design Your Plan

Tailor benefits to your workforce instead of accepting the carrier's one-size-fits-all offering.

You Keep the Surplus

When claims come in under projections, that money stays in the captive, not the carrier's balance sheet.

You Eliminate Hidden Charges

No premium taxes. No risk charges. No carrier profit margin baked into your rates.

The Network

Care from your own. At the lowest possible cost.

A $350 consult costs your practice $150 to deliver. Inside the network, plan members pay cost, not retail. That's $200 in savings on a single visit, with no negotiation and no carrier in the middle.

These are not discounts. They are the structural difference between retail pricing and actual cost of care. Every service inside the network lowers plan expenses for everyone. Live in Phoenix, Dallas, Manhattan (KS), and Tulsa.

Domestic Network

Your employees get care at your own facility at cost. Zero copay, zero deductible, zero coinsurance. Plan expense drops. Revenue stays in-house.

Friends & Family Network

Care from any member practice at cost, not retail. A $350 consult becomes $150. A procedure billed at $8,000 retail might cost $3,200 to deliver. The savings compound with every practice that joins.

Prescription Drugs

Collective purchasing power for every prescription.

The captive's collective buying power drives down Rx costs across the board. You stop paying retail. You stop paying the PBM's hidden spread. Transparent pricing, formulary control, and group leverage that a single small practice could never achieve alone.

Risk

Protected against the unexpected.

The SurgRE captive provides a stop-loss layer. Catastrophic claims are absorbed by the collective, not your practice alone. Financial stability and predictability without giving up the savings of self-funding.

Better benefits. Lower cost. Total control.

The structure exists. The captive is built. The network is live. The question is whether your practice is in it.
