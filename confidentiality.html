Practice Economics Reports

Practice Economics Reports prepared by MedMerge are confidential documents prepared exclusively for the individual or entity that requested them. Reports are based on information provided by the requesting party and on MedMerge's proprietary modeling methodology.

Reports may not be reproduced, distributed, or disclosed to third parties without MedMerge's prior written consent. Reports are not intended for public distribution and do not constitute a public offering of any product or service.

Specialist and Partner Network

MedMerge works with a network of specialists across captive insurance structure, hospital property, medical malpractice, workers' compensation, and employee benefits disciplines. The identities of participating specialists and partners are confidential. MedMerge has agreed not to use the names or marks of participating specialists without their explicit written permission.

Institutions evaluating a material engagement with MedMerge may request verification of specialist participation under a mutually executed non-disclosure agreement. Contact Info@MedMerge.co to initiate that process.

Client Information

MedMerge does not publicly identify its clients, participating facilities, or the results achieved at specific facilities without the explicit written consent of the client. Aggregate results and anonymized metrics may be presented for illustrative purposes.

Mutual Confidentiality

Information shared with MedMerge in connection with a Practice Economics Report request or a business evaluation is treated as confidential and used solely for the purpose of that engagement. MedMerge does not share client-provided information with third parties except as described in our Privacy Policy.
