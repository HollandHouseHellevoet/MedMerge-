About

We built MedMerge because the alternative was watching practices overpay forever.

We started by working with physician-owned hospitals — building coordination infrastructure, captive insurance structures, and collective purchasing networks. What we kept seeing was that small practices were getting crushed.

They were locked into fully-insured plans with no leverage, no claims data, and no alternatives. PEOs took control. Brokers took commissions. Carriers took everything else. The practices that needed the most help had the fewest options.

So we built a single employer platform. MedMerge becomes the employer of record and leases employees back to the practice. The practice keeps full operational control. And every practice — starting at one employee — gets access to self-funded benefits through the SurgRE captive and the Friends & Family network.

The Friends & Family network was designed with a simple premise: start with the cheapest possible prices. No copays. No deductibles. No coinsurance. When plan members receive care from another member practice, the savings are intrinsic. The more the network grows, the more everyone saves.
