About

We built MedMerge because the alternative was watching practices overpay forever.

The Thesis

Why MedMerge Exists

MedMerge was built on a simple conviction: independent medicine delivers better care at a lower cost.

The lower cost is not accidental. It is structural.

CMS itself pays differently depending on where care is delivered. Perform a treatment in a physician procedure room and the physician receives an enhanced professional payment with no separate facility fee. Move that procedure to an ambulatory surgery center and the facility reimbursement is still lower than the hospital outpatient department. Move it into the hospital and the price rises again.

Same physician. Same procedure. Different building. Different price.

Independent medicine has the better cost structure. Health systems have the better capital structure. That is the problem MedMerge was built to solve.

Large health systems operate at scale. They access tax-advantaged capital, spread risk across thousands of employees, negotiate purchasing agreements, and build enormous balance sheets.

Traditional medical practices were built to practice medicine and distribute professional income. They were not designed to accumulate institutional capital. Money comes in, expenses get paid, earnings are distributed to the partners. Meanwhile, the health system down the street keeps building its balance sheet. It buys another practice. Another building. Another surgery center. Another piece of the healthcare dollar.

Over time, the physician becomes economically weaker even while delivering care more efficiently. MedMerge exists to change that equation.
The Mechanism

Turn Expenses Into Assets

Independent practices already spend enormous amounts on health benefits, workers' compensation, property and casualty insurance, malpractice, and purchasing. Why should physicians finance everyone else's balance sheet instead of building their own?

Self-Funded Benefits

Practices retain the economics of good plan performance rather than transferring the entire healthcare dollar to an insurance carrier.

Member-Owned Benefits

Physicians participate in underwriting profit and investment income from risks they were already paying someone else to insure.

Direct Contracting

Employers and patients connect directly with independent physicians instead of routing healthcare dollars through hospital-controlled networks.

Purchasing at Scale

Independent practices access economics historically reserved for much larger organizations.

The objective is not merely to reduce expenses. It is to convert expenses into capital.

The Model

Scale Without Consolidation

For decades, physicians have been told that the only way to obtain scale is to surrender independence.

Sell the practice. Join the hospital. Take private equity. Become an employee.

We reject that premise.

Physicians need the advantages of scale. They do not need to give up ownership to get them.

MedMerge gives independent physician practices the financial infrastructure of a health system without requiring them to become one.

Keep the practice. Keep the autonomy. Keep the equity. Aggregate everything else.

Every practice that joins strengthens the economics of the practices already inside the network. More lives create purchasing power. More physicians create contracting power. More premium and risk create better opportunities for capital formation.

The network becomes stronger without requiring the individual physician to become less independent.

Independence. Amplified.

Independent physicians are not losing because they practice inferior medicine. They are losing because the organizations acquiring them have spent decades building financial infrastructure around healthcare while physicians focused on delivering healthcare.

Better benefits. Better risk structures. Better purchasing. Direct contracts. Capital formation.

The benefits of scale without surrendering ownership. That is why MedMerge exists.
